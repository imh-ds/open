Each job is capped at 180 minutes, with the fit step capped at 160 minutes to allow evidence upload. One aggregate job is capped at 20 minutes. **Total job-time ceiling: 3 × 180 + 20 = 560 runner-minutes** (9 hours 20 minutes summed across jobs, not elapsed waiting or a monetary quote). No automatic fits/retries, larger runners, or capacity purchases. A timeout remains an incomplete case; it does not authorize a rerun, extra budget, or removal from the three-case denominator.

The installed-package run must import from site-packages, retain the input/config hashes and exact configuration, and reload the saved bundle through the strict package loader. Preserve runtime lock, assessment ledgers, warnings, fit counts, depths, complete outputs and initial/final receipts. Retrieve artifacts/logs and save them alongside repository evidence after completion; 30-day Actions retention is temporary storage.
